What we do

Three things. Done properly. Explained in plain English.

We work with plumbers, electricians, builders and other trades in Derby and across the East Midlands. We start by looking at where you stand, then tell you straight what’s worth doing.

01Service

Get you found on Google Maps and in search.

When someone types ‘electrician near me’ or ‘builder Derby’, Google shows a map and three businesses. This is the work that gets you into that spot and keeps you there. It’s what agencies call local SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation. We just call it getting you found.

What you get

  • Your Google listing set up properly: the right categories, services, service area, hours and photos
  • Help getting more reviews from real customers, and replies to the ones you get
  • A post on your listing every week
  • Your business listed, with the same details, on the other directories that matter
  • A check on where you rank, every month

What you do

Answer a few questions at the start, send us photos of your work when you have them, and ask happy customers for a review. That’s it.

02Service

Fix your website so it brings in calls.

Plenty of trade websites look fine but don’t make the phone ring. They’re slow, they don’t say what you do or where, and the number’s hard to find. We fix that, whoever built it.

What you get

  • Clear wording that says what you do and where you work
  • A fast site that works on a phone
  • Pages for your services and the towns you cover, so Google can match you to those searches
  • A call button on every page
  • The behind-the-scenes basics Google looks for

What you do

Tell us what work you want more of, and check the wording before it goes live. That’s it.

03Service

A simple website if you haven’t got one.

A Facebook page isn’t enough. Google needs a proper website to send people to, and customers want to see you’re a real business. We build a straightforward site that does the job.

What you get

  • A clean, fast website for your trade, built to be found on Google
  • Your services, the areas you cover, photos of your work and a call button
  • A contact form that comes straight to your email
  • Set up in your name, so you own it

What you do

Send us your logo if you have one, some photos and a list of your services. That’s it.
